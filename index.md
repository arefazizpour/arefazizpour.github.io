## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Drexel University. My research focuses on computer vision and AI-generated media forensics, with an emphasis on robust detection, attribution, and adaptation to emerging generative models.

My broader research interests lie in developing reliable and adaptive AI systems that can operate under distribution shifts and rapidly evolving generative technologies.

## Research Interests

Computer Vision · AI-Generated Media Forensics · Open-World Machine Learning · Anomaly Detection · Self-Supervised Learning
